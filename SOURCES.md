# Sources and resolution notes

Files are ordered newest first. Source links identify the original file page or the available publication reference. An unavailable source is explicitly noted. Pixel dimensions alone do not establish a variant's authenticity or native resolution.

## Collection limits

- Smaller banners and title crops remain where no larger exact version was recovered.
- Alternate compositions may have different proportions and resolutions.
- Some older title logos are fan-made or have unconfirmed provenance.
- Direct wallpaper ZIP downloads for six recent updates could not be retrieved during collection. Available wiki original-file mirrors and matching artwork were used; uninspected ZIPs may contain other variants.
