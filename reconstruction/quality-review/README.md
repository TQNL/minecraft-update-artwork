# Reconstruction quality review

The current collection contains **20 reviewed derivatives alongside 48 unchanged original source files**. The latest adopted refinement updates five images: both Tricky Trials versions, the Trails & Tales panorama and both expanded Aquatic banners. No new AI generation was used for this refinement.

Tricky Trials and the panorama receive restrained sharpening in softer source detail. Aquatic combines the original expanded side artwork with its native centre, a small inherited AI title-removal water fill, and mild detail and sharpness-transition adjustments. Its source-specific bubble treatments and resolution differences remain. The titled banner shares the same scene with its existing custom title overlay.

## Review scope and limits

All 20 full compositions and targeted native detail crops were inspected. The five adjusted outputs were compared against their starting pixels for halos, detail loss, hard adjustment boundaries and title changes. The resulting images have been adopted for the reconstruction collection. Sharpening changes edge contrast, not the amount of available source detail; enlarged dimensions do not establish native resolution. Original files remain beside derivatives in each update folder.

The three earlier independent critic reviews are retained as historical records. Their scores refer to the image hashes recorded at those stages, and do not constitute a new score for subsequently changed images. No additional critic round was used for the latest refinement.

Private slides, manual inventory work, local experiments, temporary comparison pages, checkpoint PNG duplicates, inspection boards, masks and logs are excluded from the published collection. The gallery presents the adopted images with their source lineage and current methods. Original artwork and historical review records remain available.
