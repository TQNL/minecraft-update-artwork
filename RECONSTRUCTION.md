# Reconstruction branch

The [ai-reconstruction branch](https://github.com/TQNL/minecraft-update-artwork/tree/ai-reconstruction) contains separately identified derived artwork. The default branch remains the source collection.

## What changes globally

- P27: superseded Caves & Cliffs II title treatment removed.
- P33: custom Nether title treatment removed.
- P44 and P46: Exploration title crops replaced by S52, the 16:9 trailer reference.
- Primary source scenes are marked in the catalogue and gallery. This is a curatorial preference, not a claim about official publisher ranking.
- The Trails & Tales panorama is classified separately as promotional artwork.

The [PDF asset mapping](ASSET-MAP.md) preserves the review's original numbers. Removal means removal from current collection files, not a rewrite of Git history.

## Derived artwork

Source artwork stays separate from reconstructions. Derivative entries identify source IDs, the work performed and limitations. Custom title placement remains unofficial, even where it uses official lettering. Synthesized or enhanced pixels do not establish the existence of a higher-resolution original.

Work proceeds newest to oldest, one update at a time. Direct registration and source pixels are preferred wherever they suffice. AI edits are restricted to the requested reconstruction or enhancement. Every result is checked at full view and in detail for seams, duplicated subjects, distorted geometry, changed lettering and lost content.

All 24 families through Combat Update have been assessed, producing 20 derived images. See the [results and quality limits](reconstruction/RESULTS.md).
